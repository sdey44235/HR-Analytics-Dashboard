# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
👨🏻‍⚖️HR Analytics: Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights Dashboard
A dynamic, interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze employee attrition and workforce composition — focusing on department-wise trends, salary bands, job roles, and demographic breakdowns.

--> The HR Analytics Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to help HR managers and business leaders track and understand employee attrition across a workforce of 1,417 employees. The dashboard focuses on key metrics like attrition rate, department-wise headcount, salary slabs, job satisfaction, and experience trends. This tool is intended for use by HR analysts, managers, and leadership teams who need a data-driven view of retention risk and workforce health.

1. TECH STACK:
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
  📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
  📄 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer used to remove null values, duplicate records, and fix           inconsistent text values before modeling.
  🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures such as Attrition Rate %, dynamic KPI cards, and       conditional logic.
  📐 Data Modeling – Relationships established among employee, department, and satisfaction tables to enable cross-        filtering and aggregation.
  📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

2. Data Source:
   HR_AnalyticsDataset.csv (imported and cleaned in Power Query).
The dataset contains 1,480 employee records across 7 departments (Administration, Sales, Operations, IT, Marketing, Human Resources, Finance) and 9 job roles where Each record covers 36 attributes.

3. Features:
Business Problem
    HR teams lack a consolidated way to see where and why attrition is happening across departments, roles, and pay      bands.
Goal of the Dashboard
    Give HR leaders one interactive view of headcount and attrition trends to quickly spot high-risk segments and        act on retention.
Walkthrough of Key Visuals--
   -KPI Cards – Total Employees, Active Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate %, Average Age, Avg. Experience.
   -Attrition by Department (donut) and Department-Wise Employee Count (bar).
   -Attrition by Salary Slab and Attrition by Job Role & Satisfaction (matrix).
   -Age Group Distribution and Attrition by Gender.
   -Attrition Trend by Experience (area chart).
   -Slicers – Age Group and Department, filtering all visuals interactively.

4. Business Impact & Insights:
   -Turns scattered HR records into a single live view, cutting down manual reporting time for HR teams.
   -Helps leadership spot high-attrition departments, roles, or pay bands early, before turnover becomes a budget        or talent-pipeline problem.
   -Supports data-driven retention planning
   -Gives leadership a quick, filterable snapshot for board/management reporting instead of static spreadsheets.
   -Improves workforce planning — headcount and demographic views help with succession planning and diversity            tracking.
